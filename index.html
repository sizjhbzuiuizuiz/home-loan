Eligibility Criteria

  • ✓ Pakistani citizen holding a valid CNIC
  • ✓ First-time homeowner (no property in own name)
  • ✓ Age: 25–60 years (salaried) or up to 65 years (self-employed)
  • ✓ Minimum gross monthly income: PKR 25,000
  • ✓ Clean credit history (ECIB check)
  • ✓ One subsidized home loan per individual

Required Documents

  • ✓ CNIC copy of applicant
  • ✓ Proof of income: Salary slip or bank statements
  • ✓ Last 6–12 months bank statement
  • ✓ Property documents (if plot already owned)
  • ✓ Passport-sized photographs
